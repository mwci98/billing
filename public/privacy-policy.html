Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

QPOS is a business point-of-sale and management application. This policy explains how QPOS handles information when businesses use the web app, installed app, and related services.

Information we process

QPOS processes the information a business enters to operate its workspace, including account details, store settings, staff access, products, inventory, customers, suppliers, orders, invoices, payments, and reports. Where enabled by the business, QPOS may also process device camera input for barcode scanning and data required to print receipts.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, provide billing and inventory features, keep business records, support reporting, improve reliability, prevent misuse, and respond to support requests. QPOS does not sell personal information or use customer data for advertising.

Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only with services needed to operate QPOS, such as hosting, authentication, cloud storage, email delivery, payment processing, and printing integrations. These providers process data only to provide their services. A business may also choose to share an invoice or receipt using its own communication tools.

Data retention and security

Business data is retained for as long as the relevant workspace remains active or as needed for legitimate operational, legal, and accounting purposes. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Workspace owners can manage staff access and business records within QPOS. To request access, correction, deletion, or help with data associated with QPOS, contact us using the address below. Requests may need to be verified to protect the business account.

Children

QPOS is a business application and is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when QPOS changes. The current version and its revision date will always be available at this page.
